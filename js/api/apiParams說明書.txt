###
GET {{host}}/users?
  filter[createdAt][$gte]=2022-08-05 08:52:52&
  filter[createdAt][$lt]=2022-08-05 08:52:53
搜尋 createdAt >= 2022-08-05 08:52:52 並且 createdAt < 2022-08-05 08:52:53

  populate[]=roles&

  filter[username][$in][]=dust120125&
  filter[$and][account][$notNull]&
  filter[$or][username][$eq]=admin&
  filter[$or][account][$ne]=admin&
  filter[isActive]=false

  $or
